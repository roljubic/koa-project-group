The Problem

Why Hawaii Renovations
Go Wrong for Off-Island Owners

  • 01

    No One On-Site to Hold Contractors Accountable

    Without a trusted eye on the ground, contractors take shortcuts, miss milestones, and bill for work not done. You won't know until your next visit.

  • 02

    Change Orders That Spiral Out of Control

    Hawaii renovations routinely end up 30–60% over budget due to unchecked change orders. An owner's rep reviews every single one before it's approved.

  • 03

    Timelines That Stretch for Months

    Without regular milestone enforcement, Hawaii projects drag. What was supposed to be 4 months becomes 9 — and you're paying carrying costs the entire time.

  • 04

    Quality Issues Found Too Late

    Poor waterproofing, bad tile work, substandard finishes — discovered after final payment, when it's too expensive to fix properly.

"We saved a client $120,000 in unjustified change orders on a single Kahala renovation. That's what an advocate on the ground is worth."

Koa Project Group acts as your eyes, voice, and shield throughout every renovation. We know Hawaii's trades, permit processes, and market rates — and we use that knowledge to protect you at every stage.
